EZ Эдийн засаг

A Mongolian-language newsroom on the economy and world markets. Briefings, live prices, a YouTube show, lessons, and a newsletter in one place — designed and built end to end.

Mongolian readers follow copper, coal, the tögrög, and the Fed, but most of the coverage is in English or reads like a translation. EZ explains what the numbers mean here, in Mongolian that sounds like it was written in Mongolian.

The look is calm on purpose: monochrome pages, ruled lists, big type, and one accent for what is live. The markets desk puts the Mongolian Stock Exchange next to world indices, currencies, and commodities, with a calendar of releases explained in plain words.

Behind it is an editor app readers never see. Journalists draft, editors review and schedule. A daily news agent scans Mongolian and world sources and files leads for the desk — nothing goes live until a person writes it and an editor signs off.
