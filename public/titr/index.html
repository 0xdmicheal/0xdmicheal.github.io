Every room a station needs

Preview, then take

Operators cue a graphic, check it on preview, and take it to program on cue.

  • States, not just on and off

    Full, headline and compact versions of one graphic, switched without a cut.

  • Update live

    Edits to something on air wait until you send them.

  • Ready-made templates

    Lower third, name super, ticker, scorebug, countdown, statistic card, station bug, title card.

From the journalist's desk to the strip

Journalists write headline packs. The director approves them and runs the newsroom strip.

  • A desk for each role

    Journalist, producer and director desks, each with only what that person needs.

  • Approvals

    Nothing a journalist writes reaches the screen before a director approves it.

  • Live data

    Exchange rates, weather and a ticker feed, without touching a template.

Platform

One system instead of
three that don't talk

  1. 1

    One login for the station

    Five roles decide which rooms each person sees, from the journalist to the director.

  2. 2

    Layers, not one flat picture

    Video, graphics and the newsroom strip each sit on their own layer and change without touching the others.

  3. 3

    One program, any output

    The same program goes to NDI, DeckLink SDI, a screen window, OBS or vMix.
