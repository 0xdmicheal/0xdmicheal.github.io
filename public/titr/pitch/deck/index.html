The
Problem
Three machines, three logins

News, graphics and playout run on separate tools that never share data. Every headline is typed again at each step, and each step adds a typo.

Nothing joins them up

No preview, no approval and no record of who put what on air. Rates and weather are typed by hand and go stale.

Too expensive for small stations

Broadcast graphics and playout systems are priced for national networks: licences, dedicated hardware and yearly support a local station can't afford. That is why we are building Titr.

Our
Solution
Newsroom

Journalists write headline packs. The director approves them and runs the newsroom strip, live on air.

Graphics

Cue a graphic, check it on preview, take it to program. Templates are built without code.

Playout

The CasparCG engine keys it all over video and sends one program to NDI, SDI, OBS or vMix.

From Story
to Air
Write · Approve · Take

A journalist types the headline pack once. The director stamps it, and it rides to the strip on air. Nothing reaches the screen without approval, and every take lands in the as-run log.

Live
Market Data

Exchange rates, gold, copper and markets feed straight into the ticker and statistic cards. When a price moves, the flap on air flips with it: up in green, down in red, with no one retyping it.

Values from the app's feed on 13/11/2026; movement in the web version is a preview. Official Монголбанк rates are being connected; until then rates are marked ЖИШЭЭ on air.
