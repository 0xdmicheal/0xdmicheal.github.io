The
Problem

Local stations run news, graphics and playout on separate tools that never share data, so every headline is typed more than once.

In the Newsroom

Headlines are retyped from the newsroom into graphics and again into playout. Rates and weather are typed by hand, and go stale.

In the Control Room

No preview, no approval and no record of who put what on screen, while each tool brings its own licence and login.

Titr
13/11/2026
Our
Solution
Newsroom

Journalists write headline packs. The director approves them and runs the newsroom strip, live on air.

Graphics

Cue a graphic, check it on preview, take it to program. Templates are built without code.

Playout

The CasparCG engine keys it all over video and sends one program to NDI, SDI, OBS or vMix.
