Insomnus started in August 2025 as a hackathon idea: a sleepless dungeon you escape one run at a time. We won first place, launched a closed beta on Somnia mainnet, and signed a legal partnership with the network.

The pictures had to do the selling. Cave worlds, the pet lab, the crystal chamber, the skull mark, the summoning posters — a campaign system that could live next to the game, not a moodboard that never shipped.

Digital marketing and viral campaigns took registered users past 195,000. MegaETH grants. X1 EcoChain. Gate Labs as technical advisor. Docs: docs.insomnus.xyz.
