Nomin Shaglaa

Handmade gear for shagai harvaa, Mongolian knucklebone shooting — leather cases, carved rails, and the boxes the game is played from. A mark, a product shoot, and a catalogue that sells it.

Role
Mark, retouching & catalogue design
Delivered
Logo, 60+ product shots, poster catalogue
Field
Warm sand · Mongolian script

Catalogue

Product

Nomin Shaglaa makes the kit for shagai harvaa — the flick-and-strike game played with sheep anklebones. Hand-tooled leather cases, sandalwood rails, embroidered pouches, carved boxes. Craft that deserved better than phone photos.

The mark puts the ger, the moon, the sun, and the fire of the soyombo over the initials, drawn rough like a stamp. Every product was shot on one warm sand field and retouched by hand so the leather, the brass, and the wood grain read true.

The catalogue is a poster system: one card per set, Mongolian name on top, a price in the corner, and the mark and script in the margin. Enough of a frame to post straight to Facebook and still look like the same shop.
