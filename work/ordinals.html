Ordinals let people inscribe NFTs directly onto Bitcoin. No side chain, no extra database — the file lives on the sat. Satoshi Punks was one of Mongolia’s first Bitcoin Ordinals collections. At Numad Labs I owned brand, storytelling, and the NFT community from the first frame to the last mint. It sold out. Top sale $13,500. Eight BTC of volume.

Pepepunks was the spin-off: 1,350 pieces on cursed and recursive inscriptions, rare sats, two cultures in one trait sheet. I handled the marketing, the KOL map, and the campaign frames that made “normal vs cursed” readable in a second. It sold out in two hours.

Graphic work in service of a drop: trait sheets, story frames, social kits, and a world people could join before they minted.
