Brand book

Shosha

Brand guidelines for Shosha: a stacked wordmark, one signal yellow, and a system for mystery boxes, draws and battles.

Role
Brand book
Year
2026
System
Mark, color, type, voice

Shosha runs on one idea: everyone wins. The wordmark stacks SHO over SHA in a heavy, condensed cut, and yellow is the only signal colour. It marks wins, calls to action and the logo, set on near-black and nothing else.

The book covers the mark and how it can't be bent, the light and dark versions, a palette with one accent, a type system with Cyrillic support, and the voice lines that carry the brand in Mongolian.
