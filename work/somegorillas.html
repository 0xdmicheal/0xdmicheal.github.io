More of the world

Some Gorillas needed to feel like a cast, not a trait spreadsheet. Bold line, loud wardrobe, one silhouette you can read in a pfp. I built the characters and the brand so the collection had a voice before it had a listing.

The collection runs on eight bodies — the classic ape, bear, Frankenstein, zombie, skeleton, a see-through body, and three furs — with ten one-of-ones drawn on top. Every trait had to work at pfp size and on a poster.

Head or Butt is the coin flip at the centre of the app: a gold coin with a gorilla on one side and, well, the other. The stickers, the weekly task posters, and the Mainnet Arc run kept the community coming back between drops.
