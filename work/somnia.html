Identity system

Somnia Network

Design system for an L1 startup — logomark, wordmark, token marks, and the purple field the brand lives in.

Role
Identity & brand graphic
Delivered
Logo system, token marks
Field
Purple · white ink

Somnia needed a mark that reads at 24 pixels and on a stage. The system is a pair of straps around an S, a wordmark that can sit with or without them, and roundels for SOMI and USDSO. Purple is the field. White is the ink.

This is identity work for a startup — the files have to ship into campaigns, docs, and a live chain, not sit in a brand PDF.
